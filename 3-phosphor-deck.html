Quick answer

Asked 11 s ago
You asked
“How cold does the base need to be before I spin it?”
SourcedTwo references, both read just now2
Atlas answered

Chill the base to four degrees Celsius or colder, then age it at least four hours. Overnight is better — you get less ice and a smoother overrun.

The exact lines it read
Ageing the mix for 4 h at 4 °C or lower improves whipping quality and imparts a smoother body and texture.
BookIce Cream, 7th ed. — Goff & Hartel, p. 212Retrieved 11 s ago
Mix shall be cooled to 7 °C (45 °F) or below within two hours of pasteurization and held at that temperature until frozen.
RegulationMissouri DHSS — Frozen Dessert Plants, 19 CSR 20-1.030Retrieved 11 s ago
The regulation sets the floor. The book sets the target. Atlas gave you the book number and did not average them.
