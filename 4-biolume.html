square · pricing

Square for Restaurants pricing, 2026

sourced · 1

Square for Restaurants is sold in three tiers. Free covers a single location with the basics. Plus adds coursing, floor plans, and shift reporting. Premium is quoted per business and is aimed at groups above ten locations.

Plus is $69 per month per location. Card processing stays at 2.6% + 10¢ for tapped, dipped, and swiped cards, and 3.5% + 15¢ for keyed-in cards, on every tier.
this is the passage atlas quoted back to you

The processing rate does not fall as volume rises, which is the trade most small shops weigh: no negotiation, no surprises, and no discount at scale. For a shop running under roughly $30,000 a month, the flat rate usually beats an interchange-plus contract once the monthly minimums and statement fees are counted.

hardware

The Square Reader is $59 and pairs to a phone. The Square Stand for iPad is $149 and requires you to bring your own tablet. The Square Register is $799 outright, or $39 a month for two years, and includes a customer-facing display.

Hardware is not locked to a contract. A shop can start on a phone and a Reader, then add a Register in a second semester without migrating data or renegotiating anything.

offline behaviour

Card payments taken while offline are queued and processed when the terminal reconnects, with a 24-hour ceiling. Payments that fail after reconnection are your loss, not Square’s, which matters for a stand at a football game with thin coverage.
